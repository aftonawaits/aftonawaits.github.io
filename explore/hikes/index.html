Hikes near Afton, Virginia

Trailheads near the house, from a flat riverside loop to a full day on the Appalachian Trail.

  • Park · 10 min

    Rockfish Valley Foundation Natural History Center

    A small natural history center beside the valley trails.

  • Hike · 12 min

    Blue Ridge Tunnel (east trailhead)

    Walk under the mountain: an 1850s railroad tunnel, nearly a mile long and pitch dark. Bring headlamps. It’s cool and damp inside all year.

  • Hike · 12 min

    Rockfish Valley Trail

    Flat riverside walking in the valley. The main loop is about two miles.

  • Hike · 21 min

    Three Ridges

    Wilderness ridges with a view south to The Priest. A long, hard day.

  • Hike · 25 min

    Humpback Rocks

    A steep mile up to a wide view.

  • Hike · 27 min

    Dripping Rock

    Where the Appalachian Trail crosses the Parkway.

  • Hike · 27 min

    Calf Mountain

    Open meadows on the Appalachian Trail. Park entrance fee.

  • Park · 29 min

    Sherando Lake

    A lake with a beach and trails in the national forest. Day-use fee.

  • Hike · 31 min

    White Rock Falls

    An out-and-back walk to a waterfall.

  • Hike · 31 min

    Fortune’s Cove Preserve

    A nature preserve with moderate to difficult trails.

  • Park · 37 min

    Mint Springs Valley Park

    A county park near Crozet with hillside trails.

  • Hike · 38 min

    Turk Mountain

    Rock scrambles and a big view. Park entrance fee.

  • Hike · 45 min

    Crabtree Falls

    One of the tallest sets of cascades east of the Mississippi. Bring boots.
