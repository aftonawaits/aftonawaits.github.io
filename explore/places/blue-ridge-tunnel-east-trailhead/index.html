Blue Ridge Tunnel (east trailhead)

Walk under the mountain: an 1850s railroad tunnel, nearly a mile long and pitch dark. Bring headlamps. It’s cool and damp inside all year. It is about 12 minutes’ drive from the house.

There is a second trailhead on the west side of the mountain, on the Waynesboro end, if you would rather start there.
