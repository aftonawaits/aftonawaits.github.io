The tee-time weekend

Play all day. Finish here.

Most golf trips mean one course and a hotel bar. From here you can play a different course every day: mountain golf at Wintergreen, Old Trail in Crozet, Birdwood at Boar’s Head and Meadowcreek in Charlottesville are all within reach. Then you come back to your own pool, a gas grill and a fire table instead of a lobby, with three bedrooms so nobody is sharing a pull-out. Quiet hours start at 10, which suits an early tee time.
