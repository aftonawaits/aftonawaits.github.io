The game weekend

Game day. Home by the fire.

Charlottesville is about 35 minutes away, so you can skip the game-day hotel scramble and the hunt for parking near a room you barely use. The grown-up takes the king suite and the kids get the two queen rooms, with a whole house to come back to instead of a lobby. In September the pool is still open; after any game there is the fire pit and the hot tub. Football in the fall, basketball through the winter.

Why this house works for a game weekend

  • A room that’s yours

    The king suite has its own bathroom and a door that closes. The kids are at the other end of the house.

  • The fire pit

    Where the game gets replayed, play by play, long after the final whistle.

  • Pancakes before, snacks after

    A full kitchen with an island, so game day starts at home and nobody waits in line for breakfast.

  • The hot tub

    Open in every season, which matters most in basketball season.

A game day, start to finish

  1. Morning

    Pancakes at the island

    Everybody fed before anybody has to find their jersey.

  2. Late morning

    Drive in early, tailgate

    Charlottesville is about 35 minutes away. Leave early and let the kids soak it up.

  3. Game time

    Kickoff

    Football at Scott Stadium in the fall, basketball at John Paul Jones Arena through the winter.

  4. After

    Back to the house

    Out of the traffic and home to your own driveway, with free parking at the door.

  5. Evening

    Fire pit and the replay

    Chairs in a circle, the game on your phone, and the ridge going dark behind you.

  6. Night

    Hot tub, lights out

    Quiet hours start at 10. The kids will be asleep before then.

Who sleeps where

  • The grown-up: the king suite

    A king bed, a fireplace, a soaking tub and its own bathroom, at one end of the house.

  • The kids: the two queen rooms

    One each side of a shared full bathroom, at the other end. Two to a room, or a room each.

  • An extra friend: the air mattresses

    Two twin air mattresses are in the closet if somebody brings a friend. Bring linens for those.
