The Route 151 weekend

Taste there. Stay here.

The house is on Route 151 itself, in the middle of the Rockfish Valley, so the wineries, breweries, cidery and distilleries are minutes from the drive, not a day trip. Taste through the afternoon, then bring the bottles home to a long farm table, a real kitchen and a fire table. Nobody has to drive far, nobody has to find a late dinner in town, and everything after the last stop is a few steps from a bed.
