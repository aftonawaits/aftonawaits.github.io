The trail and snow weekend

Out early. Back tired.

Stay on the mountain and you are on the mountain for everything: the same few restaurants, resort prices and a condo. Stay here and Wintergreen is a short drive up for skiing, tubing or hiking, while the whole valley is at the bottom of your drive for dinner: barbecue, pizza, breweries and wineries along Route 151. You get the slopes in winter and the Blue Ridge Parkway trails the rest of the year, then a whole house, a hot tub facing the ridge and a fire table to come back to.

Close to the mountain, not stuck on it

  • Wintergreen, without living there

    Ski, tube or hike at the resort by day, then come down to a whole house, a real kitchen and your own hot tub.

  • The valley is your dinner menu

    Barbecue, pizza, breweries and wineries along Route 151, instead of the same few places at the top of the mountain.

  • The hot tub

    Open in every season, facing the ridge you were just on.

  • One level, and somewhere to dry out

    No stairs for tired legs, a washer and dryer for wet layers, and a car port to unload under.

A Saturday, trail or snow

  1. Dawn

    Coffee, boots, go

    The ridge is the first thing you see from the porch.

  2. Morning

    On the mountain

    In winter, the slopes at Wintergreen. The rest of the year, a trail off the Blue Ridge Parkway. Ideas are just below.

  3. Midday

    Lunch with a view

    A Parkway overlook does the job.

  4. Afternoon

    Down off the mountain

    Gear off under the car port, then a brewery or a late lunch in the valley.

  5. Dusk

    Hot tub

    With the ridge going dark in front of you.

  6. Night

    Fire, then bed

    The fire table outside, the gas fireplace in. Quiet hours start at 10.
