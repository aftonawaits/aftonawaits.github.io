Something for everyone

Skiers ski. Everyone else picks tubing, a spa morning at Keswick, a winery or a book by the fire. Everyone’s back in the hot tub by sunset.

How close it all is

  • 27 min to Wintergreen. Lifts, tubing and lessons for every level, from first-timers to first chair.
  • 11 min to the wineries. Veritas has a tasting room built for a cold afternoon.
  • 3 min to a brewery. Brewing Tree is practically next door.
  • 8 min to dining. Belties, Iron and Ale and Three Notch’d are all in Nellysford.
  • 35 min to Charlottesville. C&O, Mockingbird and the Downtown Mall.
  • And at home: the hot tub’s steaming and the fire pit’s lit.

Close by, boots on

Drive times are by road from the house. Tap Add and it goes into your plan.

  • Iron and Ale

    The step up after a hot shower: a chef-driven bistro at Stoney Creek, at the foot of Wintergreen. Book ahead on ski weekends.

    10 minAdd

  • Blue Ridge Tunnel (east trailhead)

    Walk under the mountain: an 1850s railroad tunnel, nearly a mile long and pitch dark. Bring headlamps. It’s cool and damp inside all year.

    12 minAdd

  • Devils Backbone Basecamp Brewpub

    Their motto is “Drink Outdoors Together”: award-winning lagers, wood-fired food and a big meadow at the foot of the mountain. Built for trading ski and trail stories.

    13 minAdd

  • Humpback Rocks

    A steep mile up to a wide view.

    25 minAdd

  • Ravens Roost Overlook

    One of the best views on the northern Parkway: 180 degrees over the Shenandoah Valley from 3,200 feet. Go for sunset, and watch for the ravens.

    Skiing and tubing in winter, trails the rest of the year.
