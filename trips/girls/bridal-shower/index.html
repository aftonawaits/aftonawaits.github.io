What the group will ask

Is this a party house?

No, and that is the appeal. Six registered guests, no parties or events, quiet hours from 10 PM to 7 AM. It suits a long dinner better than a loud one.

Can other guests join us for the day?

The house is for its six registered guests. If your plans involve anyone else, ask before you book rather than after.

Who shares a bed?

Everyone, if six of you come. There are three beds for six guests: one king and two queens. Two twin air mattresses are in the closet if someone would rather not share; bring linens for those.

When is the pool open?

May through September. It is saltwater, private to your group, and fenced.

Can we cook?

Yes. The kitchen is fully equipped, the island seats three, the table seats six, and there is an outdoor kitchen with a gas grill and a sink on the patio.

How far is it?

About 35 minutes from Charlottesville. Route 151, with its wineries, breweries, cideries and distilleries, is right outside.

Is there parking for everyone?

Yes, free parking on the property, at the door.

This is a house for six registered guests. There are no parties or events, and quiet hours begin at 10 PM. It is the right place for an intimate shower weekend, and the wrong one for a crowd.
