The girls’ weekend

Nobody’s in charge. Finally.

This house bends to whatever the weekend is for. For a wedding, the king suite becomes the getting-ready room, with its own bathroom, a soaking tub and a door that closes, and the valley’s vineyards a few minutes away. For a mahjong weekend, the long farm table takes the tiles, and from May to September there is a mahjong table that floats in the pool. For a birthday or a long-overdue catch-up, there are tasting rooms down the road, a hot tub facing the ridge and a fire pit for afterwards. Six of you, one house, and nobody else’s schedule.

Six people, three beds, and two twin air mattresses for anyone who’d rather not share. Wineries in under 10 minutes.

A Saturday, your way

  1. Morning

    Coffee on the porch

    Slowly. The ridge is right there.

  2. Late morning

    Pick your own adventure

    Hair and makeup in the king suite, a first round of mahjong, or out to the tasting rooms. Choose a driver before the first glass.

  3. Afternoon

    Back to the water

    The saltwater pool from May to September, the hot tub the rest of the year.

  4. Evening

    Dinner at the long table

    A full kitchen and a gas grill, or bring something back from the valley.

  5. Later

    Fire pit

    Chairs in a circle and the sky going dark. Quiet hours start at 10.

  6. Bedtime

    A door each

    Three bedrooms: a king and two queens.
