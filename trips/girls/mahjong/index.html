The girls’ weekend · Mahjong retreat

A weekend of tiles and very little else.

A farm table for the long sessions, a shaded porch for the afternoon ones, and, from May to September, a mahjong table that floats in the pool. Four winds and two spectators.

What the group will ask

Do you supply a mahjong set?

Yes. A mahjong mat, tiles and the pool float are available upon request. Ask when you book.

Where do we play?

The farm table seats six with the mountain in the doorway. The covered porch is in the shade. From May to September, the pool.

What about the floating table when the pool is closed?

The pool is open May to September, and the floating table goes with it. From October to April you play at the farm table or on the covered porch, and the hot tub and the fire pit are on between sessions.

Who shares a bed?

Everyone, if six of you come. There are three beds for six guests: one king and two queens. Two twin air mattresses are in the closet if someone would rather not share; bring linens for those.

When is the pool open?

May to September. It is saltwater, private to your group, and fenced.

Can we cook?

Yes. The kitchen is fully equipped, the island seats three, the table seats six, and there is an outdoor kitchen with a gas grill and a sink on the patio.

How far is it?

About 35 minutes from Charlottesville. Route 151, with its wineries, breweries, cideries and distilleries, is right outside.

Is this a party house?

No, and that is the appeal. Six registered guests staying overnight, no day guests, quiet hours from 10 PM to 7 AM. It suits a long dinner better than a loud one.
