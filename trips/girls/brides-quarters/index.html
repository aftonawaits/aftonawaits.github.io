The bride will be found here, unhurried.

What the group will ask

Is there room to get ready?

The king suite’s bath has a double vanity with two mirrors and a soaking tub under a window. The house is on one level, so nobody carries a dress down stairs. A hair dryer is provided.

Can hair and makeup come to us?

Ask when you book. The house is kept for its registered guests, and we will tell you what works on a wedding morning.

Are there stairs?

None. The whole house is on one level.

Who shares a bed?

Everyone, if six of you come. There are three beds for six guests: one king and two queens. Two twin air mattresses are in the closet if someone would rather not share; bring linens for those.

Is there parking for everyone?

Yes, free parking on the property, at the door.

How do we get in?

There is a keypad on the door. The code and arrival details are sent before your stay.

Can other guests join us for the day?

The house is for its six registered guests. If your plans involve anyone else, ask before you book rather than after.
