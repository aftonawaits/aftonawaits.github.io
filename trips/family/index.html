The kids need a pool.

Good. It’s fenced, it’s saltwater, and there’s room for six of you.

The family weekend

Everybody in the pool.

With kids, the house is the holiday. The pool is yours alone and fenced, the grill and the fire table are a few steps from the water, and everything sits on one level, so you can cook dinner and still see who is in the deep end. When you do want an outing, an orchard, a walk-through railway tunnel and ice cream are all a short drive away. It is the opposite of a hotel room and a shared pool with a queue for the loungers.

Why this house works for a family

  • A pool that’s only yours

    Saltwater, private to your family, and fenced all the way round with a gate. Open May to September.

  • Everything is steps apart

    Pool, grill, table and fire table sit together behind the house, so you can cook and still see the water.

  • One level, no stairs

    Nothing to gate off and nobody to carry up. A gas fireplace and a television for the rainy hour.

  • A kitchen for six

    Stocked with the basics, an island for pancakes, and a dishwasher for afterwards.

A Saturday, start to finish

  1. Morning

    Pancakes at the island

    A full kitchen with the basics already in the cupboards. Somebody small gets to flip.

  2. Late morning

    Cannonball o’clock

    Straight out the back door to the pool.

  3. Lunch

    Off the grill

    The outdoor kitchen is steps from the water, so nobody has to dry off to eat.

  4. Afternoon

    One outing

    An orchard, a tunnel you can walk through, or ice cream. Ideas are just below.

  5. Evening

    Fire table and stories

    Chairs around the fire as the ridge goes blue. Quiet hours start at 10.

  6. Bedtime

    Lights out

    A king and two queens, all on one hallway.
