The family weekend

Everybody in the pool.Hot tub’s on. Fire’s lit.

With kids, the house is the vacation. The pool is yours alone and fenced, the grill and the fire pit are a few steps from the water, and everything sits on one level, so you can cook dinner and still see who is in the deep end. When you do want an outing, an orchard, a walk-through railway tunnel and ice cream are all a short drive away. It is the opposite of a hotel room and a shared pool with a line for the lounge chairs.

Why this house works for a family

  • A pool that’s only yours

    Saltwater, private to your family, and fenced all the way around, with a gate. Open May to September.

  • Everything is steps apart

    Pool, grill, table and fire pit sit together behind the house, so you can cook and still see the water.

  • One level, no stairs

    Nothing to gate off and nobody to carry up. A gas fireplace and a television for the rainy hour.

  • A kitchen for six

    Stocked with the basics, an island for pancakes, and a dishwasher for afterwards.

Close by, and good with kids

Drive times are by road from the house. Tap Add and it goes into your plan.

Outside

  • Blue Ridge Tunnel (east trailhead)

    Walk under the mountain: an 1850s railroad tunnel, nearly a mile long and pitch dark. Bring headlamps. It’s cool and damp inside all year.

    12 minAdd

  • Chiles Peach Orchard

    Pick-your-own in season, and something sweet at the counter.

    20 minAdd

  • Monticello

    Thomas Jefferson’s mountaintop home. Make a day of it: the house in the morning, then lunch at Michie Tavern down the hill. Book a timed ticket.

    38 minAdd

  • Crabtree Falls

    Big waterfalls, and the first overlook is a short walk from the parking lot. The full climb is steep, so turn back whenever the little legs say so.

    45 minAdd
