10:00 AM Out in the valley

Go out. Everything is close.

  1. Route 151

    The house sits just off Nelson County’s Brew Trail: a single road strung with breweries, wineries, cideries and distilleries. Pick two. Come back for the third tomorrow.

  2. Blue Ridge Parkway

    The Parkway begins at Rockfish Gap on Afton Mountain, a short drive up the valley. Overlooks every few miles, no trucks, no hurry.

  3. Shenandoah National Park

    Skyline Drive starts at the same gap and runs north along the crest. Bring boots.

  4. Wintergreen

    The mountain resort up the valley: skiing and tubing in winter, trails and golf the rest of the year.

  5. Hiking

    The Appalachian Trail runs along the Blue Ridge above the valley. Guests mention the hiking as often as the hot tub.

  6. Charlottesville

    About thirty minutes east, for dinner, the Downtown Mall, or a rainy afternoon.

Questions

When is the pool open?

May through September. It is a private saltwater pool, used only by your group.

How many people can stay?

Six, in three bedrooms: one king and two queens. There are also two twin air mattresses (bring linens), a pack ’n play and a booster seat.

Is it good for children?

Yes. The pool is fenced, the house is on one level, and there is a pool basketball hoop and a lot of lawn.

Are there stairs?

No. The home is a single level.

Can we bring our dog?

We’re sorry, no pets. Violations are fined.

How far is everything?

Route 151 is right outside. Charlottesville is about 30 minutes. The Blue Ridge Parkway and Shenandoah National Park both begin at Rockfish Gap on Afton Mountain, a short drive away.

How do we get in?

There is a keypad on the door. The hosts send your code and arrival details before your stay.

Which fireplaces work?

The living room has a gas fireplace and the king bedroom has an electric one. Outside, the round stone patio has a fire at its centre.
